Computational Linguistics in Pakistan

An Introductory Panel and Community Launch

Date: 4 February 2026

Time: 8:30 – 9:45 PM (Pakistan Time)

Mode: Online (Live Session)

This event marks the first official activity of the Computational Linguistics Society of Pakistan (CLSP). The panel introduces the scope of Computational Linguistics in Pakistan, discusses current research in NLP and language technologies, and formally launches a national academic and professional community.

Panelists

  • Muhammad Shoaib Tahir
  • Dr Tafseer Ahmad
  • Dr Husnain Raza
  • Dr Muntazar Mehdi
  • Ms Sadia Ashraf

Moderator: Meesum Alam
