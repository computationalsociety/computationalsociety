Computational Linguistics in Pakistan

An Introductory Panel and Community Launch

Date: 4 February 2026

Time: 8:30 – 9:45 PM (Pakistan Time)

Mode: Live Online Event

This event marks the first official activity of the Computational Linguistics Society of Pakistan (CLSP). The panel introduces the field of Computational Linguistics in Pakistan, highlights current work in NLP and AI, and formally launches a national academic and professional community.

Panelists

  • Muhammad Shoaib Tahir
  • Dr Tafseer Ahmad
  • Dr Husnain Raza
  • Dr Muntazar Mehdi
  • Ms Sadia Ashraf

Moderator: Meesum Alam

All are welcome.
