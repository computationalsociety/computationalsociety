Why Join the Society?

Membership in the Computational Linguistics Society connects you with researchers, educators, and professionals working at the intersection of language and computation.

  • ✔ Access to members-only academic resources
  • ✔ Participation in workshops, seminars, and conferences
  • ✔ Networking with linguists and NLP practitioners
  • ✔ Opportunities for collaboration and visibility
  • ✔ Support for early-career researchers and students
