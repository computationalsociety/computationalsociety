About the Society

Advancing research and collaboration in Computational Linguistics

Who We Are

The Computational Society is an academic and professional organization dedicated to advancing research, education, and collaboration in Computational Linguistics, Natural Language Processing, and Language Technology.

Our Mission

We aim to promote linguistically grounded AI, support early-career researchers, and foster interdisciplinary collaboration between linguistics, computer science, and cognitive science.

What We Do

  • Organize academic workshops and conferences
  • Support student and researcher networking
  • Promote ethical and responsible language technologies
