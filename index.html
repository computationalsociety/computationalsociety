Computational Linguistics Society

An academic society dedicated to advancing research, education, and collaboration in Computational Linguistics, NLP, and language technologies.

About the Society

The Computational Linguistics Society brings together researchers, educators, students, and professionals working at the intersection of language and computation. We promote linguistically grounded, ethical, and socially responsible language technologies.
