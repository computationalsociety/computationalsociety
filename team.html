Executive Team

Our leadership brings together expertise in computational linguistics, language technologies, and interdisciplinary research.

Muhammad Shoaib Tahir

Computational Linguist

Muhammad Shoaib Tahir is a computational linguist with expertise in computational linguistics and language-focused technologies. He is the author of Stats in Corpus Linguistics: A Guide Book (2025) and a contributing author of the book chapter “Natural Language Processing with Corpora” (forthcoming).

His expertise includes corpus system development, Python programming, Firebase, Google Apps Script, and Linux-based systems. He currently serves as Assistant Manager HR at the Linguistic Association of Pakistan and is affiliated as a Lecturer at COMSATS University Islamabad, Lahore Campus.

Meesum Alam

Fulbright PhD Scholar

Meesum Alam is a Fulbright PhD scholar in Computational Linguistics at Indiana University Bloomington. His work focuses on language documentation and NLP for low-resource and Indigenous languages of Pakistan.

His research includes speech and text dataset development, morphological analyzers, Universal Dependencies, and community-driven language technologies to promote linguistic equity and digital inclusion.

Husnain Raza

Computational Linguist

Husnain Raza is a data-driven computational linguist working at the intersection of technology, discourse, and political communication. His expertise includes data mining, annotation, applied machine learning, conversation design, and social media research.

His research addresses propaganda, disinformation, and conspiracies using corpus linguistics, discourse analysis, social network analysis, and computational methods, with a focus on building FAIR and large-scale datasets.

Mehak

Computational Linguist

Mehak is a linguist specializing in computational linguistics and language modeling. She holds a master’s degree from the University of Siena, Italy, and developed the Sindhi-Reasoning-Instruct dataset.

Her expertise includes corpus creation, data extraction, annotation, and linguistic analysis, bridging theoretical linguistics and computational applications for low-resource languages.

Sadia Ashraf

PhD Scholar (Computational Linguistics)

Sadia Ashraf is a former Fulbright Scholar and a PhD student in Computational Linguistics at Indiana University Bloomington, with a minor in Cybersecurity Risk Management.

Her research lies at the intersection of AI, cyber policy, and harmful speech in digital spaces, with a strong focus on under-resourced Pakistani languages and responsible language technologies.
